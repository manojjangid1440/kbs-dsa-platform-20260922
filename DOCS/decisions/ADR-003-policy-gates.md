# ADR 003 Unresolved policy fails closed

Status: accepted engineering choice. Date: 2026-09-22.

Missing approved policy or provider configuration returns an explicit unavailable/requires-review outcome. Pure core functions accept explicit versioned policy values supplied by trusted services. No production commission seed, automatic HDFC activation synonym, assumed training reactivation duration or fake verification response.

Engineering simulation fixtures are synthetic and separate. A production service cannot obtain policy approval from a user-submitted boolean. Domain tests exercise mechanics with fixture configurations, not claimed business sign-off.
