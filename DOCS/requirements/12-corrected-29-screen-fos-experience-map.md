# 12. Corrected 29-screen FOS experience map

Authority: source PRD; full section reproduced, not an overview. Source paragraph range P0202–P0233.

**[P0203](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0203)** The original FOS draft lists 29 conceptual screens. Preserve their functional coverage but interpret S21-S27 according to the MIS-only bank-status rule. The product may combine adjacent screens for an intuitive UI as long as all required information and actions remain available. No visual screen sequence is authority to change bank status.

**[P0204](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0204)** Original screen | Required experience and correction

**[P0205](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0205)** S01 Welcome / Onboarding | KBS branding, introduction, Skip/Next; no approval promises.

**[P0206](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0206)** S02 Credit Card Product Range | Bank/NBFC branding and Admin-published credit card range.

**[P0207](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0207)** S03 Digital Selling | Explain digital customer lead creation, issuer-link initiation and MIS-based tracking.

**[P0208](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0208)** S04 Credit Card Promotion | Approved marketing content; no guaranteed approval.

**[P0209](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0209)** S05 Sell More, Earn More | Advisor value proposition and Get Started without inventing payout rates.

**[P0210](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0210)** S06 Mobile Number | Advisor mobile entry, privacy/terms/consent.

**[P0211](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0211)** S07 OTP Verification | OTP, masked number, resend/edit and validated expiry; exact digit count and timer are implementation choices unless configured.

**[P0212](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0212)** S08 Credit Card Home | Cards, Create Lead, My Leads, actual Pending Actions, notifications, payout links.

**[P0213](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0213)** S09 Credit Card Catalogue | Cards with image, bank, key benefits and fee highlights.

**[P0214](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0214)** S10 Credit Card Categories | Travel, Shopping, Premium/Top, Fuel and configured categories.

**[P0215](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0215)** S11 Card Search and Filters | Search issuer/card and meaningful business filters.

**[P0216](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0216)** S12 Credit Card Details | Image, issuer, benefits, fees, disclosures, available Admin link, Create Lead.

**[P0217](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0217)** S13 Customer Mobile | New operational lead begins with customer's mobile number.

**[P0218](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0218)** S14 Customer Details | Required basic information, correction and validation.

**[P0219](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0219)** S15 PAN | PAN capture and actual verification result; avoid unnecessary exposure.

**[P0220](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0220)** S16 Pincode / Location | Residence pincode with verified city/state or 'unavailable'.

**[P0221](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0221)** S17 Employment / Income | The three specified employment types and annual income as per ITR.

**[P0222](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0222)** S18 Declarations | Explicit required declarations and Credit Bureau acknowledgement.

**[P0223](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0223)** S19 Review and Submit | Accurate summary; back/edit before submitting internal lead.

**[P0224](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0224)** S20 Lead Created | KBS reference and next step; no implied bank submission/decision.

**[P0225](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0225)** S21 Eligibility / Card Result | Only genuinely available confirmed sourcing/eligibility; no inferred result.

**[P0226](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0226)** S22 Application Initiation | Share/open chosen issuer link; record only KBS operational action, not a bank-reported stage.

**[P0227](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0227)** S23 Pending Actions | Display real MIS-supported or explicitly logged operational tasks, never fabricated tasks.

**[P0228](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0228)** S24 My Credit Card Leads | Show separate real bank stage, decision, activation, bank remarks and MIS date.

**[P0229](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0229)** S25 Lead Search and Filters | Customer/reference search; card, bank, stage, decision, activation, date filters.

**[P0230](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0230)** S26 Lead Detail | Bank MIS current values and separate operational history; next action only if supported.

**[P0231](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0231)** S27 Application / Lead Timeline | Replace old fixed progression  with chronological MIS updates and separate KBS activity log.

**[P0232](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0232)** S28 Notifications | MIS changes, genuinely actionable tasks, payout updates and company notices.

**[P0233](../source/prd-verbatim.md#p0233)** S29 FOS Profile | Profile, reporting code, payout/bank summary, support and logout.
